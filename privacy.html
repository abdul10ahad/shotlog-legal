ShotLog Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

ShotLog helps you keep a log of your GLP-1 treatment: your shots, how you feel and your progress between doctor visits. Your health log stays on your iPhone. ShotLog has no account, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in ShotLog's own storage. This includes your medication and dose plan, the shots you log, injection sites, check-ins (weight, symptoms and how you feel), lab results, supply counts, visit dates and your notes. We don't receive any of it and have no server that stores it.

Like any app's data, it's included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups if you have them turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple under your Apple Account.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, ShotLog saves the weight from your check-ins to Apple Health. ShotLog never reads anything from Apple Health. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared with anyone.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. ShotLog only learns whether the check succeeded. It never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Reminders

Shot and check-in reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They aren't sent from a server.

Backups and reports you create

When you export a backup, ShotLog encrypts it with a password you choose (AES-256) and saves it wherever you pick. We can't open your backup or recover a lost password.

Visit reports are created on your iPhone as PDFs. They go only where you choose to share them.

Purchases and Superwall

ShotLog Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles your payment, and we never see your card or bank details. To show the right purchase screen and check whether Pro is unlocked, ShotLog uses Superwall, a paywall service. Superwall receives:

  • a random identifier that ShotLog creates on your device (not your name, email or Apple Account),
  • basic device details, such as your iPhone model, iOS version, language, region and app version,
  • which purchase screens you saw and which buttons you tapped there,
  • your App Store purchase and subscription history for ShotLog.

Superwall never receives your health log, weight, shots, symptoms or any other content you enter. We use this information only to run purchases in the app, not to track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices and rights

Deleting ShotLog from your iPhone deletes everything stored in the app. You can also delete your data at any time from within the app's Settings. To access or delete the purchase information held by Superwall, or to use any other right you have under data protection law (including the EU GDPR), email us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

ShotLog isn't intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
